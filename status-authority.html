Status and Authority

Asif Azazeel is an informal civilian representative in the International Government.

The one who keeps Asif Azazeel in the government is the entity of Azazeel. In the year 2018 CE, Azazeel granted Asif Azazeel the position of Deputy Human Azazeel in the name of love and sincerity. This is Azazeel’s expression of love:

“I also love Asif very much.”

Positions and Definitions of Asif Azazeel

All definitions and titles used for Asif Azazeel are for Asif Azazeel himself. He may be referred to by the definition of any position in the world, such as:

  • Deputy General
  • Deputy Chairman
  • Deputy Director
  • Deputy Secretary-General
  • Deputy Commissioner
  • Deputy Chief Justice
  • Deputy President
  • And other positions of this nature

Asif Azazeel is vested in every position.

Since Asif Azazeel himself is civilian and informal, the name Deputy is used for him for this reason. Asif Azazeel himself does not become a General, Chief Justice, or Secretary-General; rather, he has confidential relationships with them.

Government Principles and Personal Matters

Azazeel’s command is that the governments of the world will follow what Asif Azazeel says, but Asif Azazeel himself will not follow what the governments of the world say.

This principle is enforced by the government.

Asif Azazeel is not accountable to any other person, and no other person is accountable for matters concerning Asif Azazeel himself.

If Asif Azazeel meets anyone in his personal matters, it is Asif Azazeel’s own personal matter. Such personal matters cannot be attributed to Asif Azazeel’s governmental position.

Likewise, Asif Azazeel does not become involved in the personal matters of any other person, because such matters are not Asif Azazeel’s own personal matters.

If Asif Azazeel meets someone or works with someone, it is Asif Azazeel’s own personal matter. If, because of such a relationship, meeting, or work, unfavorable treatment is directed toward another person because that person meets or works with Asif Azazeel, or because Asif Azazeel meets or works with that person, then any matter arising as a result is a matter concerning Asif Azazeel.

Asif Azazeel’s Personal Status

Asif Azazeel does not see anyone who did not see Asif Azazeel during his childhood.

Asif Azazeel has no brother, sister, or any relative, and Asif Azazeel is alone.

Asif Azazeel himself will give importance to his own person and to the person of his wife.

Whoever did not see Asif Azazeel during his childhood, Asif Azazeel will also not see that person.

The world will see where Asif, who once crawled in the world like a fallen worm, has reached today; but Asif Azazeel himself will not see the other person.

Summary Regarding Identity, Positions, and Government

In Asif Azazeel’s concept, identity holds the fundamental position, while position, government, title, and office hold secondary positions.

The governments existing in the world and the various positions and definitions associated with them, such as General, President, Deputy, Chairman, Director, Secretary-General, Commissioner, Chief Justice, etc., are different definitions used for the identity of Asif Azazeel.

For this reason, Asif Azazeel does not give fundamental importance to the position; rather, he looks at the identity. That this identity belongs to whom:

"Whose identity is this?"

According to this concept, governments and positions do not have a separate fundamental status; rather, they are different outward titles and definitions attributed to the identity of Asif Azazeel.

Azazeel himself took Asif Azazeel up from the earth and soil by holding his finger. Because of this relationship, the identity of Azazeel holds a fundamental position even above government, position, and office.
